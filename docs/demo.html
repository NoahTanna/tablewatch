This is a demo copy of the host-stand screen. It runs in your browser with the same rules as the real app. There's no camera: a simulated dinner service plays on its own, or you can play the camera for any table with the buttons below.

Timers are sped up: a table turns red 20 seconds after guests leave dishes behind (real app: 3 min), and orange "WAITING" after 30 seconds with nothing served (real app: 10 min). Tap a red or orange table to say it's been handled. Tap Sound off to hear the chime.
