Host stand screen: three directions

Same restaurant, same moment on a busy Friday, three ways to show it. Each one is a working mockup at iPad size: timers tick, and you can tap a red table to mark it cleared or an orange one to confirm someone has been there. Pick the one you'd want at your host stand, or tell me what to take from each.

Example data: 14 tables, a made-up dinner service. Nothing here comes from a real restaurant. "Eating" replaces "Served" as the stage name in all three, because that's how a host talks about a table.
